Personal Advisor Services Overview

Vanguard Personal Advisor Services (PAS) combines low-cost Vanguard funds with personalized financial planning and ongoing portfolio management. This page summarizes the three service tiers, fees, eligibility, and the planning process.

1. Service Tiers

TierMinimumAnnual FeeAdvisor Model
Personal Advisor $50,000 0.30% on balances up to $5M Team-based — any CFP® advisor in the pool
Personal Advisor Select $500,000 0.30% on balances up to $5M Dedicated CFP® advisor
Personal Advisor Wealth Management $5,000,000 Tiered: 0.30% on first $5M, 0.20% on next $5M, 0.10% on next $15M, 0.05% above $25M Dedicated team (CFP® + tax + estate specialists)

The advisory fee is in addition to the underlying Vanguard fund expense ratios. Most PAS portfolios use index funds with weighted-average expense ratios of approximately 0.07%–0.10% (see VG-ED-002 Expense Ratio Explainer).

Total cost example: A $1M Personal Advisor portfolio would pay approximately 0.30% advisory + 0.08% fund expenses = 0.38% all-in, or about $3,800 per year. Industry comparable advisors typically charge 1.0%–1.5% all-in.

2. What Personal Advisor Services Includes

Financial Planning

  • Retirement planning (cash-flow projection, withdrawal strategy)
  • Tax planning (Roth conversion strategies — see VG-OP-006)
  • Estate planning coordination (POA, beneficiaries — see VG-OP-002, VG-OP-013)
  • College funding (529 strategy)
  • Debt management and emergency fund analysis
  • Insurance coverage review (recommendations only; Vanguard does not sell insurance)

Investment Management

  • Portfolio construction using low-cost Vanguard funds (typically VTMSI/VTMSA/VTMS, VTBMI/VTBMA/VTBM, and international counterparts)
  • Quarterly rebalancing to target allocations
  • Tax-loss harvesting at the lot level (Wealth Management tier; opt-in for other tiers)
  • Asset location optimization (placing tax-inefficient holdings in tax-advantaged accounts)
  • Withdrawal sequencing for retirees

Ongoing Service

  • Annual portfolio review (more frequent for Select and Wealth Management)
  • Ad-hoc planning meetings (unlimited)
  • Secure messaging with your advisor
  • Quarterly performance reports beyond standard statements (see VG-OP-017)

3. Eligibility and Onboarding

To enroll, you must be:

  • Age 18 or older
  • A U.S. resident (some non-resident exceptions for select tiers)
  • Holding eligible assets at Vanguard (or willing to transfer in)

Eligible assets include:

  • Vanguard mutual funds and ETFs
  • Non-Vanguard ETFs and stocks held in Vanguard Brokerage
  • Externally held assets transferred in (typically within 4–6 weeks of enrollment)

Onboarding Process

  1. Discovery call — 30–60 minutes; assess goals, time horizon, risk tolerance, household balance sheet
  2. Plan delivery — written financial plan within 1–2 weeks
  3. Account setup — open advised accounts, transfer in external assets if applicable
  4. Implementation — initial portfolio construction; first rebalance
  5. Ongoing — quarterly check-ins, ad-hoc reviews

4. Investment Philosophy

Vanguard PAS portfolios reflect the firm's core investment principles:

  1. Goal-aligned allocation. Portfolio mix matches your financial-planning horizon and goals.
  2. Broad diversification. Global equity and fixed-income exposure across thousands of securities.
  3. Cost discipline. Default to index funds; active funds used selectively.
  4. Discipline over prediction. No tactical market timing.
  5. Tax awareness. Tax-efficient asset location and harvesting.

The default building blocks for most portfolios are the Total Stock Market Index Fund (VG-PR-001), Total Bond Market Index Fund (VG-PR-003), and their international counterparts.

6. Tax Reporting

PAS-managed accounts receive the same year-end tax forms as self-directed accounts: 1099-DIV, 1099-B (with cost basis tracked per VG-OP-007), and 1099-R for IRAs. See VG-OP-010 (Tax Document Guide) for full detail.

7. Cancellation

You may cancel PAS at any time without penalty. Upon cancellation:

  • Advisory fees stop on the cancellation date (prorated)
  • You retain ownership of all underlying funds
  • Accounts remain open at Vanguard as self-directed brokerage or mutual fund accounts
  • You may re-enroll later if eligibility persists

8. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Will my advisor recommend non-Vanguard funds?
PAS portfolios are built using Vanguard funds. External holdings transferred in may be retained or liquidated based on the plan's tax efficiency analysis.

Q: Can my advisor manage my 401(k)?
Generally no — 401(k) plans must be managed within the plan provider's platform. PAS may offer guidance on 401(k) allocation as part of overall planning.

Q: How often will I talk to my advisor?
Personal Advisor: typically once a year plus on-demand. Personal Advisor Select: quarterly check-ins typical. Wealth Management: relationship-driven cadence, often monthly.

Q: Are advisors fiduciaries?
Yes. PAS advisors are SEC-registered investment advisers acting as fiduciaries.

Q: Can I keep some accounts self-directed while having others advised?
Yes. You can mix advised and self-directed at the same firm.

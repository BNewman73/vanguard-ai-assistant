## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how Vanguard treats accounts that show prolonged inactivity, including outreach steps and the eventual escheatment (transfer to state) of unclaimed property under state law.

## 2. Definition of "Inactive"

An account is considered **inactive** when:
- No client-initiated activity for **24 consecutive months** (no logins, no contributions, no withdrawals, no address updates), AND
- Returned-mail flag set on at least one statement, OR
- Failed contact attempts at all phone numbers and email addresses on file

Inactivity status is reviewed monthly by Vanguard's Unclaimed Property team.

## 3. Outreach Sequence

Once flagged as inactive, Vanguard initiates a structured outreach sequence:

| Stage | Timing | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | At inactivity flag | Mailed letter to last known address |
| 2 | +30 days | Email outreach (if address on file) |
| 3 | +60 days | Outbound call to all numbers on file |
| 4 | +90 days | Letter via Trusted Contact Person (if designated — see VG-OP-013) |
| 5 | +180 days | Skip-trace search via third-party investigator |
| 6 | Ongoing | Annual re-review until matter resolved |

## 4. Escheatment Process

If outreach fails and the dormancy period under applicable state law is met, the account is **escheated** — assets are turned over to the state's unclaimed property division. State dormancy periods generally range from 3 to 5 years.

For most retirement accounts (IRAs, 401(k)s), state dormancy laws apply but with special care: states may not be able to maintain the tax-deferred character of an IRA after escheatment, so Vanguard prioritizes locating account holders before this stage. Escheated retirement assets may trigger an unexpected taxable event for the original owner.

The state's unclaimed-property division holds escheated assets until a rightful claimant retrieves them; most states impose no time limit on retrieval, but proof of identity and connection to the original owner is required.

## 5. Reactivation

To reactivate an inactive (but not yet escheated) account:
1. Log in at investor.vanguard.com (forces password reset and identity verification if dormant 12+ months)
2. Update contact information
3. Confirm beneficiary designations (see VG-OP-002)
4. Optionally, designate or update Trusted Contact (see VG-OP-013)

If the account has already been escheated, reactivation is not possible at Vanguard. The account holder must contact the state's unclaimed-property division to retrieve assets.

## 6. Decedent Accounts

If the inactivity is due to the death of the account holder:
- Estate, executor, or beneficiary should contact Vanguard at **800-555-0199** with a **certified death certificate**
- Estate Settlement procedures begin (see VG-OP-002, Section 7)
- For RMD obligations on inherited retirement accounts, see VG-OP-003 (Section 5)

## 7. Avoiding Inactive Status

Account holders can prevent inactive flagging by:
- Logging in at least once every 24 months
- Keeping address, phone, and email up to date in the profile
- Designating a Trusted Contact Person (see VG-OP-013)
- Enrolling in e-delivery to ensure communications reach you (see VG-OP-017)

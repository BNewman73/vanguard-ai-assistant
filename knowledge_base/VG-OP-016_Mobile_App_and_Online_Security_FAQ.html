Mobile App and Online Security FAQ

This FAQ covers the Vanguard mobile app (iOS and Android) and the security controls that apply to all digital access. For broader fraud-prevention policy, see VG-OP-011; for transfer-specific protections, see VG-OP-005.

Mobile App

Q: Where can I download the Vanguard mobile app?

iOS: Apple App Store. Android: Google Play. Search "Vanguard." Always verify the publisher is "The Vanguard Group, Inc." before installing.

Q: What can I do in the mobile app?

  • View account balances, holdings, and performance
  • Place trades on Vanguard mutual funds, ETFs, and individual stocks
  • Transfer money between linked bank accounts and Vanguard accounts
  • Mobile check deposit (see below)
  • View statements and tax forms (see VG-OP-017)
  • Update profile, beneficiaries (see VG-OP-002), and trusted contacts (see VG-OP-013)
  • Secure messaging with Vanguard support

Q: What can I NOT do in the mobile app?

  • Initiate wire transfers over $10,000 (use web at investor.vanguard.com)
  • Roth conversions over $25,000 (use web or call 800-555-0155)
  • Open new accounts of certain types (e.g., trust accounts, custodial accounts)
  • Submit POA documentation (see VG-OP-013)

Mobile Check Deposit

Q: How does mobile check deposit work?

  1. Open the Vanguard app and select Move Money → Deposit Check
  2. Endorse the back of the check: sign your name and write "For mobile deposit only at Vanguard, account #_____"
  3. Take photos of the front and back of the check
  4. Enter the amount and select the destination Vanguard account
  5. Submit

Q: What are the deposit limits?

Limit TypeAmount
Per check (single deposit)$100,000
Daily total$100,000
Monthly total (rolling 30 days)$250,000

Checks above these limits must be mailed (PO Box 982901, Valley Forge, PA 19482) or deposited via wire (see VG-OP-005).

Q: How long until funds are available?

Cashier's checks, certified checks, and money orders: 1 business day. Personal and business checks: 5 business days. Vanguard reserves the right to extend holds for fraud review (typically capped at 7 business days).

Q: What types of checks are NOT accepted via mobile deposit?

  • Foreign checks (any check drawn on a non-U.S. bank or in a non-USD currency)
  • Checks made payable to a third party
  • Checks dated more than 6 months prior
  • Post-dated checks
  • Checks missing endorsement or with altered amount/payee
  • Traveler's checks

Q: What should I do with the paper check after deposit?

Write "Deposited via Vanguard mobile app on [date]" on the front, and retain the check securely for at least 14 days. After 14 days, shred and dispose. Do not redeposit elsewhere.

Logging In and Authentication

Q: What multi-factor authentication options does Vanguard offer?

  • SMS one-time code — sent to a phone on file
  • Voice call one-time code — accessibility option
  • Authenticator app — Google Authenticator, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator (TOTP)
  • Hardware security key — FIDO2 / WebAuthn (e.g., YubiKey) — strongest option
  • Biometric — Face ID / Touch ID for the mobile app (after initial pairing)
Recommendation: Use an authenticator app or hardware key as the primary second factor. SMS is convenient but vulnerable to SIM-swap attacks.

Q: When am I prompted for MFA?

  • First login on a new device
  • Login from a new location (geo-IP-based)
  • Sensitive transactions (e.g., wire transfer initiation, beneficiary change, password change)
  • Periodic re-verification (every 30 days for trusted devices)

Q: I lost my MFA device. How do I regain access?

Call 800-555-0199. After identity verification (which may include knowledge-based questions, ID upload, and possibly contact with your Trusted Contact Person), MFA can be reset. Plan ahead by registering more than one factor.

Account Security Best Practices

Q: How do I create a strong password?

  • At least 16 characters
  • Random, not based on personal information
  • Use a password manager (1Password, Bitwarden, etc.)
  • Unique to Vanguard — do not reuse passwords across financial sites

Q: How will Vanguard contact me?

Vanguard sends:

  • Service emails from @vanguard.com domains
  • SMS from short codes (e.g., 84778, 49622) — never linking to non-Vanguard URLs
  • Phone calls from the official numbers listed in VG-OP-015
Vanguard will NEVER:
  • Ask you for your full password (we never see it; it's hashed)
  • Ask you to read aloud an MFA code received by SMS — this is a known scam tactic
  • Ask you to install remote-access software (TeamViewer, AnyDesk, etc.) on your computer
  • Send you a check and ask you to wire some of it back
  • Demand immediate gift card payments
If anyone asks for these things claiming to be Vanguard, hang up immediately and call our Fraud Hotline at 800-555-0166. See VG-OP-011 for full fraud-prevention policy.

Device Management

Q: How do I see what devices have access to my account?

Web: Profile → Security → Trusted Devices. You can see device name, last login date and location, and revoke any device's trusted status.

Q: Can I disable mobile-app access entirely?

Yes. Profile → Security → Mobile Access → Disable. Web access continues to work normally.

Privacy

Q: Does the mobile app track my location?

The app may use coarse-grained location for fraud detection (matching to typical login geography). It does not track or store precise location for any other purpose. Detailed location services are off by default.

Q: How is my data encrypted?

All data in transit uses TLS 1.3. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256. Authentication data (passwords, MFA secrets) is hashed with industry-standard algorithms.

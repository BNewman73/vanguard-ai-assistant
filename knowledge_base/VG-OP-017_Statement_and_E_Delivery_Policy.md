## 2. Delivery Channels

Vanguard offers three delivery channels:

1. **Electronic delivery (e-delivery)** — default for new accounts; statements posted to investor.vanguard.com and emailed link to the account holder
2. **Paper mail** — printed and mailed to address of record
3. **Both** — e-delivery plus paper

Account holders may elect any combination per document type. For example, you may receive trade confirmations electronically but quarterly statements on paper.

## 3. Account Service Fee Waiver

Accounts with balances **below $5,000** are subject to a **$25 annual account service fee**. The fee is automatically waived for accounts that meet **any** of these criteria:

- Enrolled in e-delivery for **all** required documents (statements, prospectuses, shareholder reports, tax forms)
- Total household assets at Vanguard of **$5,000 or more** (across all accounts)
- Enrolled in Vanguard Personal Advisor Services (any tier)
- Enrolled in Vanguard Brokerage Services as a primary brokerage relationship
- Inherited IRAs and certain employer-sponsored plan accounts

E-delivery is the simplest waiver path for most account holders.

## 4. Enrolling in E-Delivery

Online: **My Accounts → Profile → Mail Preferences → E-Delivery**.

You may select e-delivery for:
- Account statements
- Trade confirmations
- Prospectuses and SAIs
- Shareholder reports
- Proxy materials
- Tax forms

Enrollment is effective immediately. The next eligible document will be delivered electronically.

## 5. Email Preferences

Vanguard sends email notifications when:
- An account statement is available
- A trade has executed (if confirmation e-delivery is on)
- A tax form is available
- A material change to your accounts is made (security setting, beneficiary, address)
- Service-related notifications (e.g., scheduled outages)

Marketing emails are separately controlled in **Email Preferences**. Service emails cannot be turned off because they reflect required regulatory communications.

## 6. Document Retention

Vanguard retains:
- Account statements: indefinitely (downloadable for 10+ years online)
- Trade confirmations: 10 years
- Tax forms: 7 years
- Beneficiary designations: indefinitely

Documents older than online retention windows are available on request via **800-555-0199**; allow 5–10 business days for delivery.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I mix paper and electronic per document type?**
Yes. Each document category has its own delivery setting.

**Q: Will I lose access to historical statements if I switch to e-delivery?**
No. All historical statements remain available online regardless of current delivery preference.

**Q: Is e-delivery secure?**
Yes. Documents are accessed through the authenticated investor.vanguard.com portal, never sent as email attachments. Two-factor authentication is required for new device logins (see VG-OP-016).

**Q: How do I print or save my e-delivered statements?**
PDF format is provided for all statements. Use your browser's print or save function.
